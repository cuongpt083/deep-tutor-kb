# 8. Making Research Easy with ChatGPT: From Pain Points to Focused Copy

## 💡 Key Takeaways
- ChatGPT can accelerate the research phase by generating a broad list of customer pain points based on a concise prompt.
- The copywriter’s critical role is to filter, evaluate, and select the single most potent pain point for the campaign.
- Focusing on one primary pain point (like a needle tip) creates deeper impact than spreading efforts across multiple diluted points.
- Structured prompts ("dive in", "keep it short") help extract deep yet concise insights suitable for copy materials.

## 📖 Detailed Content

### 1. Research Methodology with ChatGPT
The instructor introduces a second research method: leveraging ChatGPT to generate customer pain points (penpoints) during the copywriting workflow. This method is particularly effective for targeting specific audiences like non-profits/NGOs.

**Example Context:** TXT Swat, a texting tool designed for enterprise-level communication, unlike personal messengers (Messenger, WhatsApp, Zalo). It supports mass messaging with personalization (e.g., Hello Phương → Hello Land → Hello Mine).

**Prompt Workflow:**
1. **Initial Research Prompt:** 
   `We are TXT Swat. TXT Swat is a SAS texting tool targeting non-profits. Help me list pain points related to our service.`
   Result: ChatGPT generated 15+ specific pain points such as "Limit resources," "Budget constraints," "Donor engagement challenges," and "Fundraising difficulties."

2. **Deepening Prompt:** 
   `Help me dive in to the penpoint.` 
   Used to expand a selected pain point with more detail and nuance.

3. **Condensation Prompt:** 
   `Keep it short and succinct in two sentences.` 
   Why? Research yields abundant information, but copywriting requires concise raw materials. Feeding dense data into ChatGPT later (along with product benefits, differentiators) hinders its ability to generate coherent copy. Keeping insights to 1–2 sentences ensures easy digestion.

### 2. The "One-Point Focus" Principle
A core lesson emphasized that copywriters should select only **ONE** primary pain point per campaign. Analogized to a needle: if the needle has too many "balls" (pain points) attached, it cannot deliver a deep impact. Concentrating on a single, potent point (e.g., "Donor Engagement") allows the copy to strike directly and memorably.

### 3. Additional Prompt: Brand Differentiation
When researching brand uniqueness, instructors can feed full brand descriptions (e.g., a jewelry brand's "About Us" page) into ChatGPT with the prompt: `Help me point out the differentiation of this brand in one sentence.` This condenses dozens of paragraphs into a single, powerful differentiator for later use in copy.

**Ream Outcomes:** 
An alternative research angle involves shifting from "pain points" to "ream outcomes" (desired micro-goals). The same prompt structure applies: replace "penpoint" with "ream outcome" to uncover aspirational targets.

### 4. Integrating AI into the Copywriting Workflow
The ultimate goal is not to let AI write the final copy unchecked, but to use it as a research accelerator. The human copywriter gathers, curates, and condenses AI output, then combines it with product benefits, differentiators, and brand story to produce the final deliverable (email, landing page, sales page).

## ❓ Câu Hỏi & Trả Lời Trọng Tâm (Q&A for Search)
- **Hỏi:** How can ChatGPT be used to research customer pain points for copywriting? 
  - **Đáp:** By providing a clear audience and context prompt, ChatGPT can generate a list of relevant pain points. The copywriter then filters, deepens selected points with follow-up prompts, and condenses them into 1–2 sentence insights for use as copy raw materials.
- **Hỏi:** Why should a copywriter focus on a single pain point rather than multiple points in one campaign? 
  - **Đáp:** Focusing on one primary pain point creates a concentrated, persuasive message (like a needle tip), whereas multiple points dilute the impact and prevent the copy from resonating deeply with the audience. The copy becomes scattered and less memorable.
- **Hỏi:** What are the key prompts shared for researching pain points and brand differentiation using ChatGPT? 
  - **Đáp:** The shared prompts include: (1) "Help me list pain points for [audience/service]," (2) "Help me dive in to the penpoint," (3) "Keep it short and succinct in two sentences," and (4) "Help me point out the differentiation of this brand in one sentence." These can be adapted for various topics by inserting the relevant brand or audience details.
