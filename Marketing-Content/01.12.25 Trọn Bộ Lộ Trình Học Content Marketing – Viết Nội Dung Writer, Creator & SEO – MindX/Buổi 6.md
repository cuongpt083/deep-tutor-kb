# Buổi 6: Nền tảng Social Media & Chiến lược Tạo nội dung Reels, Facebook, Instagram, REST

## 💡 Tóm Tắt Cốt Lõi (Key Takeaways)
- Hệ sinh thái social media tại Việt Nam bao gồm Facebook, Instagram, TikTok và nền tảng mới REST/Threads mang tính phi tập trung, mang lại cơ hội tiếp cận và tương tác đa dạng.
- Reels định hình chiến lược video ngắn: hook 3-5 giây bắt đầu, tiếp cận non-follower, hỗ trợ kể chuyện thương hiệu và KPI view, phù hợp với thói quen người dùng tiêu thụ nội dung nhanh, rõ ràng và có logic.
- Meta Business Suite & Instagram Insights cung cấp các chỉ số then chốt (Reach, Engagement, Follower growth, hiệu năng Reels) để căn cứ vào dữ liệu tối ưu hóa nội dung và lịch đăng.

## 📖 Nội Dung Chi Tiết
### 1. Nền tảng Facebook & Instagram: Cấu trúc nội dung, Visual & Reels Strategy
Facebook content strategy hinges on three pillars: a strong hook within the first 3-5 seconds or words of the caption, strategic use of keywords/slogans/insights to align with user needs, and adherence to platform policies (restricted sectors include alcohol, tobacco, beauty, gaming, gambling; identity verification laws apply). Visually, support diverse formats (image, video, carousel, mini-game, poster, podcast) and aspect ratios (9:16 for Reels/IG, 16:9 for YouTube); while the old 20% text-overlay rule has been removed, quality scoring still impacts Reach, so format variety is recommended. Instagram Reels function as a reach and engagement engine: their short-form nature matches consumer preference for quick, clear, and logical narratives; they facilitate brand building through storytelling and community connection; and view counts serve as a primary KPI, with the algorithm favoring videos that retain viewers in the first few seconds. The 5-step Reel creation process involves: (1) Planning—defining story, context, and visuals; (2) Goal & topic specification; (3) Shooting—camera angles, product introduction, and pacing; (4) Editing—adding effects, text overlays, icons, tickers, and custom thumbnails; (5) Publishing—accompanied by a concise caption and 2-3 relevant hashtags to maximize discoverability.

### 2. Instagram Framework, Analytics & Nền tảng REST/Threads
Instagram content creation follows a 5-step framework: (1) Define content direction and brand identity to ensure consistency and creative alignment; (2) Discover valuable messages and angles that tap into user desires and pain points; (3) Create using visual assets and AI-assisted tools (Meta AI enables auto-caption, hashtag suggestions, and smart editing to accelerate production); (4) Scale reach through paid advertising, look-alike audiences, and cross-ecosystem sharing (Instagram ↔ Fanpage ↔ REST); (5) Measure performance via Insights to refine future content. The platform's writing mechanics employ distinct styles: Hooks (questions, data points, stories, or proverbs) grab attention; the Body can follow dissemination (detail-to-big), narrative flow, inductive reasoning, synthesis, parallel structure, or cliffhanger techniques; Closures direct with a CTA (e.g., "visit our store"), summarize key takeaways, or pose open-ended questions to solicit feedback. Meta Business Suite analytics offer an Overview tab tracking Reach, Engagement, Follower trends, Monetization earnings, Link clicks, and Video minutes watched. Users can schedule posts for optimal times (algorithm-suggested), publish immediately, or save as drafts. Video thumbnails can be auto-selected from footage or uploaded customy. Deep-dive insights segment Organic vs. Paid reach, top geographic regions (country/city), and top-performing content types—Reels are especially prioritized for 2025 growth.
