# Content Positioning, 70/30 Rule & Posting Time Optimization

## 💡 Tóm Tắt Cốt Lõi (Key Takeaways)
- Content should balance authentic sharing with strategic leading/directing content to build recognition and guide audiences toward conversion.
- The 70% useful + 30% selling rule ensures value delivery while enabling monetization; brand-focused accounts may sell less as audiences seek them for solutions.
- Posting time significantly impacts engagement: morning (motivational/light), lunch (sales/entertainment peak), afternoon (family/light content for interaction), evening (relaxation-optimized sales with softer conversion).
- Sustainable personal branding requires three core activities: targeted network expansion, algorithm-driven engagement, and consistent profile maintenance with niche refinement.

## 📖 Nội Dung Chi Tiết
### 1. Content Positioning & Strategy
- Positioning as a "fascinating individual" through diverse content types: lifestyle, beauty, health, family, emotional stories, and practical tutorials.
- Two content modes: "sharing" (building identity & recognition) and "leading/directing" (introducing business purpose or services after audience connection is established).
- Avoid publishing two consecutive posts with the same format within a day or on consecutive days to maintain variety and prevent audience fatigue.
- Idea generation support: the 30-day plan framework, grounded in profile status and brand identity, to structure ideas and prevent creative blocks.
- Core content formula: 70% valuable/informative content + 30% promotional/selling content. For personal-brand images, selling can be integrated freely; for solution-seeking audiences, minimal selling is needed as they already identify the value proposition.

> **Ví dụ / Case Study:** Content about pharmaceutical products should position the creator as an interesting individual through diverse sharing (gender, virtual living, beauty, household tips, family, health, emotional stories). After building recognition, leading/directing content introduces business objectives. The 70/30 rule allows 70% useful info + 30% sales; brand-focused accounts may sell less as audiences seek them for solutions.

### 2. Posting Time Optimization & Profile Growth
- **Posting Time Slots:**
  - Morning (6–8 AM): Audience seeks light, motivating content to start the day with energy. Ideal for motivational quotes, daily energy posts, and positive mindset sharing.
  - Lunch (11 AM – 1 PM): Peak browsing period; audiences are relaxed and scrolling frequently. Best for sales pitches, entertainment content, and direct promotional posts, as purchase intent is relatively higher.
  - Afternoon (4–5 PM): Post-work fatigue period; audiences browse to unwind. Content about family, food, and light entertainment resonates well and drives interaction.
  - Evening (8–10 PM): Audience is relaxed and has time to read, but decision-making is reduced. Sales content can be posted but conversion rates may be lower than lunch slots; focus on relaxation-oriented or softer promotional material.
- **Three Core Profile Growth Activities:**
  - **Network Expansion:** Target niche-relevant groups and communities rather than rapid, mass adding. Invite quality connections from relevant interactions. For accounts near or at 5,000 friends, use manual or tool-assisted (e.g., Symbol Facebook, ATB Software) cleaning to remove inactive/low-engagement friends and make room for quality targets.
  - **Engagement:** The algorithm prioritizes content from frequently interacted users. Active commenting, liking, and sharing within relevant groups and pages expands reach and builds relational equity, which supports conversion.
  - **Profile Maintenance:** Personal branding is a persistent, cumulative process. Gradual niche recognition attracts the target audience. Ongoing relationship management—connecting, nurturing, and converting per target niche—is essential for sustained growth and conversion.

## ❓ Câu Hỏi & Trả Lời Trọng Tâm (Q&A for Search)
- **Hỏi:** "How to optimize posting times on Facebook to increase engagement?"
  - **Đáp:** Recommended posting times are lunch (11h–13h) for sales content, morning (6–8h) for motivational posts, afternoon (16–17h) for family/food content, and evening (20–22h) for relaxation content; lunch is the golden hour due to highest browsing volume.
- **Hỏi:** "What is the 70/30 content rule in content marketing?"
  - **Đáp:** This rule requires 70% content providing useful, educational, or entertaining value, and 30% sales or promotional content, ensuring a balance between value delivery and conversion without annoying the audience.
- **Hỏi:** "How to expand a quality friend community for a personal brand niche business?"
  - **Đáp:** Focus on niche-relevant groups and communities, interact with posts to attract natural connections, avoid rapid mass adding, and use filtering tools like Symbol Facebook or ATB Software to remove inactive friends, keeping quality to increase conversion rates.
