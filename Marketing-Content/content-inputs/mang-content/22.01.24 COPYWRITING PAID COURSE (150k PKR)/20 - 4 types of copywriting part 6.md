# 20 - 4 types of copywriting part 6: Transformational Copywriting

## 💡 Tóm Tắt Cốt Lõi (Key Takeaways)
- Transformational copywriting focuses on purpose-driven storytelling and a journey from point A to Z.
- It prioritizes personal narratives and audience insights over entity-driven messaging.
- Integration of NLP and hypnotherapy ideas from users, audience, or customers shapes resonant, persuasive copy.

## 📖 Nội Dung Chi Tiết
### 1. Mục tiêu và nguyên lý cốt lõi
Transformational copywriting is defined by its emphasis on **storytelling** and **personal narratives**. The core objective is to guide the audience from an initial state (A) to a transformed state (Z) through a meaningful journey. Unlike entity-driven copy that pushes product features or brand attributes, this approach is **purpose-driven**, aiming to inspire internal change or perspective shifts. The instructor references a personal story (associated with "Yuli") that illustrates an individual's journey, highlighting challenges and breakthroughs as part of the narrative arc.

### 2. Áp dụng NLP, hypnotherapy và insights khán giả
When NLP (Neuro-Linguistic Programming) and hypnotherapy are referenced, the copy leverages **ideas from users, audience, or customers** to create resonant content. These ideas are curated to align with the audience's deeper motivations and subconscious patterns. The purpose-driven nature ensures the copy addresses "why" rather than just "what", fostering genuine engagement.

> **Ví dụ / Case Study:** Yuli tells a personal story about an individual's journey from A to Z, incorporating challenges encountered along the way. This narrative framework serves as a model for how transformational copy can structure content to lead the audience through a realistic, relatable, and impactful transformation.

## ❓ Câu Hỏi & Trả Lời Trọng Tâm (Q&A for Search)
- **Hỏi:** What is transformational copywriting and how does it differ from entity-driven copy?
- **Đáp:** Transformational copywriting is purpose-driven and focuses on guiding the audience through a narrative journey from A to Z, emphasizing personal stories, challenges, and internal transformation rather than pushing product features or brand attributes (entity-driven).
- **Hỏi:** How are NLP and hypnotherapy concepts applied in transformational copywriting?
- **Đáp:** NLP and hypnotherapy ideas are used to incorporate insights from users, audience, or customers, shaping the copy around purpose-driven motivations and subconscious patterns to enhance resonance and persuasion.
- **Hỏi:** Can you give an example of the A-to-Z journey framework in transformational copy?
- **Đáp:** Yes, the framework involves structuring a narrative where the audience starts at point A (e.g., a problem or mindset), encounters challenges, and reaches point Z (a transformed perspective or goal), often illustrated through personal stories like that of "Yuli" in the course.
