# What colors to use to create your Upwork profile

## 💡 Tóm Tắt Cốt Lõi
- Determine the experience you want people to have when they look at your profile.
- Define how you want them to feel.
- Clarify how you want people to think about you.

## 📖 Nội Dung Chi Tiết
### 1. Mạch Xã Hội Cá Nhân & Định Hình Bản Thân
- These three points form the foundation for selecting colors, layout, and content for personal or Upwork profiles.
- Color psychology serves as a tool to convey the intended experience, emotions, and mindset, aligning design choices with branding objectives.

> **Ví dụ / Case Study:** The lesson prompts reflection on experience, emotions, and self-perception when designing a profile, requiring thoughtful color and layout selection to match branding goals.

### 2. Áp Dụng Thực Tế cho Profile
- Start by answering the three core questions to guide color palette selection.
- Use color to evoke specific emotions and shape professional perception, ensuring consistency across visual and textual elements.

## ❓ Câu Hỏi & Trả Lời Trọng Tâm (Q&A for Search)
- **Hỏi:** "How should one define the experience, emotions, and self-perception for a professional profile?"
  - **Đáp:** The lesson identifies three core directives: (1) the experience you want viewers to have, (2) the emotions you want them to feel, and (3) how you want them to think about you. These guide all subsequent design decisions, including color selection, to maintain branding coherence.
- **Hỏi:** "Why is it important to define emotional and perceptual goals before choosing profile colors?"
  - **Đáp:** Color directly influences viewer cognition and emotion; pre-defining the three core goals ensures color choices support the intended branding message rather than relying on personal preference alone.
- **Hỏi:** "How can color be used effectively to communicate brand identity through a profile?"
  - **Đáp:** By first establishing the desired experience, emotion, and perception, one can select a color palette that consistently reinforces these elements across profile visuals, creating a strong, recognizable personal brand.
