# Jadugary Technique Ninth for Writing Sales Subheadings

## 💡 Tóm Tắt Cốt Lõi (Key Takeaways)
- The "Who am I and why you should listen to me?" subheading is a classic copywriting framework used primarily by marketers to build credibility.
- Its core purpose is to lower the audience's natural resistance, which stems from fear of receiving unfair value or being "ripped off."
- By establishing authority and trust before the main offer, the technique makes prospects more receptive to the sales message.

## 📖 Nội Dung Chi Tiết
### 1. The Classic Subheading Framework
- **"Who am I and why you should listen to me?"** is a recognized subheading structure designed to pre-frame the reader's mindset and establish immediate credibility.
- This technique is predominantly used by marketers across websites, email sequences, and sales pages as a proven conversion opener.
- The subheading explicitly signals the author's authority or relevance, directly addressing the audience's innate skepticism.

### 2. Overcoming Audience Resistance Through Credibility
- Audience resistance is a natural barrier driven by the fear of being exploited or receiving unfair value compared to other buyers.
- The primary goal of this subheading is to lower that resistance by building trust upfront, making the audience more open to the subsequent value proposition.
- Psychologically, it redirects the reader's skepticism into receptiveness, reducing the likelihood of immediate dismissal of the offer.

> **Ví dụ / Case Study:** In practice, marketers often pair this subheading with brief credentials, social proof, or authority statements to reinforce the credibility claim and amplify conversion rates. The transcript illustrates the core mechanism: establishing "why listen" before "what's in it for me."

## ❓ Câu Hỏi & Trả Lời Trọng Tâm (Q&A for Search)
- **Hỏi:** "Who am I and why you should listen to me?" copywriting subheading purpose?
  - **Đáp:** This subheading is designed to build immediate credibility with prospects, establishing the author's authority or relevance before presenting a value proposition, thereby reducing audience skepticism and resistance.
- **Hỏi:** Why does credibility need to be built in copywriting?
  - **Đáp:** Credibility is essential because audiences naturally resist sales pitches due to a fear of being exploited or "ripped off"; building trust lowers this resistance and makes them more receptive to the offer.
- **Hỏi:** Who primarily uses the "Who am I and why you should listen to me?" technique?
  - **Đáp:** This technique is mostly used by marketers and copywriters in sales pages, email sequences, and website copy to warm up the audience and prime them for conversion.
