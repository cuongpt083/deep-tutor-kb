# Inquiring Bullet Template

## 💡 Tóm Tắt Cốt Lõi (Key Takeaways)
- The lesson introduces 21 distinct types of bullets applicable to copywriting.
- These bullets are intended to make copy stand superior to competitors' and close more sales.
- Content was delivered in a Fabian Service class context, with bilingual (English/Vietnamese) transcription notes.

## 📖 Nội Dung Chi Tiết
### 1. Foundation of Bullet Usage in Copywriting
- Bullet points serve as structural elements to dissect value propositions, maintain reader engagement, and facilitate quick decision-making.
- The session emphasizes a systematic template ("Inquiring Bullet Template") for generating varied, high-impact bullets.

### 2. The 21 Bullet Types & Inquiring Framework
- A catalog of 21 bullet variants is presented, each serving a unique persuasive or psychological function.
- The "Inquiring" approach focuses on crafting bullets that pose questions or spark curiosity, prompting readers to seek answers embedded within the copy, naturally guiding them toward the offered product or service.
- Core mechanism: leverage curiosity gaps and direct inquiry to increase dwell time and conversion rates.

> **Ví dụ / Case Study:** Bài giảng sử dụng khung template bullet gợi hỏi để tạo câu hỏi và tò mò, hướng dẫn độc giả đến giải pháp bán hàng, mặc dù nội dung cụ thể bị nhiễu trong bản ghi thô.

### 3. Practical Application Guidelines
- Identify the core value or pain point to address.
- Select the appropriate bullet type from the 21-category framework.
- Frame bullets as inquiries or curiosity-provoking statements to maximize reader interaction.
- Test and iterate bullet variations for conversion optimization.
